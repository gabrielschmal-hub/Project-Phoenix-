Nvidia fell 14.01% after a report of an SEC accounting inquiry, and chip makers fell 12-14%. The Nasdaq fell 5.28% and the S&P 3.70%, which ended the week down 8.61%. The VIX rose to 35.89, while the two-year yield fell about 13bp and gold fell 2.51%. The book is flat; Q4 is +45.38 realised, heat 0R.

Governor Cook still warned that waiting risks falling behind the curve, and a December hike is about 90% priced. Spot gold fell 2.51%, a fifth straight fall.

Sigma (σ) measures a move against that instrument’s own recent behaviour, not against zero. One sigma is a normal day. Two sigma is a move bigger than roughly 95% of its last twenty sessions — rare enough to be information rather than noise.

What actually matteredone session, filtered

The AI trade broke down, and bonds became the safe place

1. Nvidia's problems turned into the sector's problem. A leadership change, a reported SEC accounting inquiry and new export rules have now cost Nvidia about 28% since 11 November. Chip makers fell 12-14% on Friday, and Trump's warning about data centres abroad hit the whole AI build-out.

2. Bonds rallied as stocks fell. The two-year fell 13bp and the ten-year 12bp, the first day in weeks that Treasuries acted as a haven. Lower inflation expectations in the Michigan survey helped. But a December hike is still about 90% priced, and Fed officials keep talking about more.

3. Forced selling showed up everywhere. Gold fell for a fifth day and silver 7.4%, and bitcoin fell below $60,000. When gold falls alongside stocks, investors are usually raising cash rather than seeking shelter.

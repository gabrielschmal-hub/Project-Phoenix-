Sigma (σ) measures a move against that instrument’s own recent behaviour, not against zero. One sigma is a normal day. Two sigma is a move bigger than roughly 95% of its last twenty sessions — rare enough to be information rather than noise.

What actually matteredone session, filtered

Nvidia's leadership question was settled; the Fed question was not

1. One of last week's main worries went away. Huang's removal was a large part of Nvidia's 28% slide, and his return reversed most of it in a day. The reported SEC accounting inquiry and the export rules are still open, and Nvidia's statement only said it will keep cooperating with government inquiries.

2. The Fed is still pointing to a December hike. Williams said there is more work to do, the two-year yield rose and hike pricing moved closer to certain. Stocks rallied on company news, not on any easing in the Fed's position. Wednesday's PCE inflation report is the next test.

3. A short-covering kind of day. The biggest gains came in the names that fell most last week, such as neoclouds, memory and AI servers. Days like this often come when sellers run out, but whether they mark a low only shows in the sessions that follow.

The war · the dayWTI (Jan) $80.17 · Brent $89.37

Attacks over the weekend, talk of talks, and crude near $80

The weekend brought more attacks. Bahrain said an Iranian strike hit a civilian industrial site, and Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they targeted US positions there. UKMTO reported attacks on shipping near Hormuz, including a tanker hit near Khasab. The Houthis claimed an attack on a Saudi refinery, which was not confirmed.

Diplomacy got more time. Trump told Fox News he may resume the war next year if talks fail, and warned that ships testing the US blockade would end up at the bottom of the sea. Qatar's prime minister said Doha is working with Iran and the US to revive negotiations.

Crude fell on supply, not on the attacks. Libya reopened its Sharara pipeline and Middle East flows rose. January WTI settled $80.17 and Brent $89.37. OPEC+ meets on 29 November to set 2027 output.

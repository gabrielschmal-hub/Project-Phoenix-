The first actual mechanism: a joint corridor, swept of mines, with a deadline attached

TALKSIran and Oman negotiate an interim framework — a temporary joint maritime corridor plus a mine-clearance project, with a permanent route targeted in 30 to 60 days. This is categorically different from last week's atmospherics. Deputy Foreign Minister Gharibabadi described technical talks covering corridor administration, information exchange and maritime security services — the plumbing of an actual reopening rather than a statement about wanting one. The strait has been largely closed since March.Rigzone · Japan Times · 2026-08-26
POLITICSTehran insists the Hormuz track is independent of US negotiations; Trump says the Navy is mine-clearing. Iran is deliberately separating the waterway from the broader war so one can move without the other — and conditions any return to US talks on Washington honouring the June deal. The analyst read is blunt: the wildcard is whether the administration backs a framework it did not broker.Rigzone · 2026-08-26
OILWTI −2.6% to 80.21, Brent under $86 — a third straight decline and roughly 8% off last Thursday's high. The premium keeps draining as the reopening path becomes specific. At 80 rather than 87 the arithmetic that has been propping the inflation gate open starts to reverse, and energy stops being the reason the regime call survives.TheStreet · 2026-08-26

The transmission is oil → inflation → the regime call, and for the first time the friendly direction has a mechanism and a clock rather than a mood. But Wednesday also showed the limit of that: core PCE came in unchanged at 3.3%, which says the inflation already in the system is not an energy artefact and will not fall just because crude does. So the regime survives this week regardless — what changes is the marginal risk. Watch for a start date on the technical talks; if the corridor gets scheduled, crude has further to give, and the war stops being the first line of this brief.

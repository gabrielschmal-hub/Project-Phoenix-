Nvidia beats and guides to $130bn — after a fourth day of losses takes the S&P to a five-month low
Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8bn, well above forecasts, and guided Q4 to $130bn; the shares rose about 4% after hours. Before that, the S&P fell 1.03% to a five-month low and the Dow 1.64%, with every sector lower and the VIX at 25.46. The book is flat; Q4 is +45.38 realised, heat 0R.
In five minutesthree things
1Nvidia beat and guided higher, and the shares rose about 4% after hours. Revenue was $115.8bn, up 103% from a year ago, against about $109.1bn expected, and adjusted EPS $2.68 against $2.48. Data-centre revenue was $107.6bn and the gross margin 74.8%. Q4 is guided to $130bn, including an extra week, against about $125.1bn expected, with no data-centre revenue from China assumed. Interim CEO Colette Kress said the company's momentum is real; there was no update on the CEO search. The shares had closed 0.44% lower at 155.46.
2Stocks fell for a fourth day, with every sector lower. The S&P fell 1.03% to a five-month low, the Dow 1.64%, the Russell 1.50% and the Nasdaq 0.74%. Only about 75 S&P stocks rose. Software led the decline, with Salesforce down 9.45%, and the VIX rose 11.13% to 25.46. Home Depot named its CFO as CEO and fell 2.45%; Disney fell 1.48% despite a beat and a 33% dividend increase.
3Mixed data and a split Fed. Retail sales rose 0.2% in October, a touch above forecast, while industrial production fell 0.4% and the homebuilders' index dropped to 25, a 14-year low. St. Louis's Musalem said up to four more hikes may be needed by spring, while New York's Williams said there is no need for dramatic action. A December hike stays about 91% priced.

The book is still flat. Palantir fell 2.39% to 156.56, well below the 194.25 exit, and AMZN closed at 257.89. Q4 stays at +45.38, +2.77R, all realised, and the day was +0.00. The full 3.0R is available.

What actually matteredone session, filtered

Nvidia answered the numbers question, not the leadership one

1. The business is still growing fast. Revenue doubled from a year ago, beat forecasts by about $6.7bn, and the Q4 guide of $130bn is about $5bn above consensus even with no China data-centre sales assumed. Nvidia returned $28.1bn to shareholders in the quarter.

2. The questions now are outside the numbers. There is no permanent CEO yet, the Wall Street Journal reported an SEC inquiry into the company's disclosures after the CEO's exit, and Commerce will require licences for advanced chip exports to five more countries from Thursday. A 4% after-hours gain after a beat this large is a modest reaction.

3. The broad market is weakening underneath. The S&P is at a five-month low, breadth was about 5 to 1 negative, software is falling hard, and housing sentiment is at a 14-year low. A Fed still talking about more hikes leaves little support.

Good morning Aldemar · Friday, 4 September 2026
Waller takes September off the table — and yesterday's Palantir story turns out to be a Google story
Fed governor Christopher Waller said he would "support keeping rates unchanged" if inflation continues to cool, and the September hike went from the market's base case to a coin flip: 54% hike against 46% hold, down from roughly two-thirds on Wednesday. Everything repriced at once — S&P +1.06% to 7,747.71, Nasdaq +1.40%, Dow +1.18%, gold up $125.30 to 4,539.90 in its largest single-day gain of the conflict, the dollar down 0.58% and the VIX to 14.32. A correction to yesterday's edition, and it matters: Palantir's 5.81% fall on Wednesday was not valuation and profit-taking. It was Google. Alphabet's DeepMind released Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber under a government-and-critical-infrastructure-only access programme, and the market read it as a hyperscaler walking into Palantir's defence stronghold. On Thursday Palantir answered with a PwC partnership and took the whole loss back, +7.71% to 182.53. Two positions in this book are now on opposite sides of the same trade.

OILWTI +0.29 to 91.30 and Brent −0.11 to 95.52 — crude went nowhere on the loudest day of the war. US pump prices are averaging $4.14 a gallon, which is the real political constraint and the reason this cannot run indefinitely. But the futures market has stopped reacting to headlines, because the escort operation keeps demonstrating throughput and because Waller just removed the channel through which oil was hurting equities.Investrade · CBS News · 2026-09-03

Three days ago the chain ran strait → oil → inflation → Fed, and every link fired. On Thursday the last link was cut: Waller made the September decision about CPI rather than crude, so a barrel of oil no longer converts directly into a rate hike. That is why the tape could rally 1% while the president declared diplomacy worthless. The war has not become less dangerous — "asymmetric" and "dark months ahead" are threats to widen it, and $4.14 gasoline is a clock — it has become less financially transmissible for the next two weeks. The thing that would restore the transmission is a successful attack on an escorted convoy. Nothing else on the board does it.

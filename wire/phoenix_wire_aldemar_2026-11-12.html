Sigma (σ) measures a move against that instrument’s own recent behaviour, not against zero. One sigma is a normal day. Two sigma is a move bigger than roughly 95% of its last twenty sessions — rare enough to be information rather than noise.

What actually matteredone session, filtered

Nvidia changed its chief executive, and the bond market caught up with CPI

1. The largest AI company lost its founder as chief executive. Huang resigned a day after the Journal's report, Kress took over on an interim basis, and the board will look inside and outside for a permanent CEO. The shares fell as much as 14% before recovering to a 4.34% loss, and they are now about 11% below where they closed on 6 November. The 17 November results will be the first under new leadership.

2. Yields reset higher. With Wednesday's holiday over, the two-year rose about 15bp and the ten-year about 12bp, pricing Tuesday's hot CPI and a run of hawkish Fed speakers. Small caps, which borrow more, fell 1.66%, and gold fell 2.79% as yields and the dollar rose.

3. Housing is feeling it. Existing home sales fell to their lowest pace since August 2010, with mortgage rates near their highest since 2000. That is the cost of the Fed's hiking path showing up in the real economy, and it is the main argument against a half-point move.

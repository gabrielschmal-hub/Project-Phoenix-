What actually matteredone session, filtered

The AI trade swung back to the suppliers, then Nvidia's leadership came into question

1. CoreWeave turned the chip mood. Its raised 2026 guide, above the $12.86bn consensus, said data-centre demand is still growing, and the whole chip index followed. That is the opposite of last Thursday, when the same names fell 10-20% on fears of tighter data-centre limits. CoreWeave gave back about 6% after hours as the Nvidia report broke.

2. Nvidia's problem is now about leadership, not a report. The board had already opened an investigation; the Journal's report that it is preparing to remove Huang is a much bigger step, and it came a week before Nvidia's results. Nasdaq-100 futures were down about 0.85% after hours.

3. The Fed is not softening. Three more officials leaned toward hikes after Tuesday's CPI, and Goldman added March to its forecast. With no bond trading on Wednesday, Thursday's session will show how yields take that.

Iran denied holding talks with the United States and said the Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until its demands are met.

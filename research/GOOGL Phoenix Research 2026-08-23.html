The business

An advertising monopoly funding a capital programme the size of a national infrastructure budget.

Alphabet sells attention and, increasingly, compute. The tension in the stock is that the first business throws off enormous cash and the second consumes it faster than it arrives — and for the first time that gap has turned free cash flow negative.

Advertising — the cash engine

Search and YouTube, roughly 68% of Q2 revenue. AI features are driving query growth rather than cannibalising it, on management's account.

YouTube reach is extraordinary: over 1.7 billion unique viewers watched World Cup content, and 140 million used Ask YouTube in June alone.

Google Cloud — the accelerant

$24.8bn, +82% year over year. Not decelerating from a high base — accelerating. Customer acquisition velocity more than doubled.

Gemini Enterprise is used by nearly 90% of the Fortune 100. That is the number that matters most in this report.

Gemini and the Bets

950 million monthly active users, daily actives tripled in a year, 22 billion API tokens processed per minute.

Waymo sits here too — Pichai described the focus as "scaling the business right now". Gemini 4 already in development, with new models shipping close to monthly.

Revenue beat and the stock fell. The capex guide came in $7–17bn above what analysts modelled, and free cash flow went negative. Ashkenazi attributed the increase to accelerated capacity delivery — demand-driven, not overrun.

How it is being funded

Alphabet is now financing growth externally, which is new for this company:

Bond issue, early August$22.93bn
Maturities2028 to 2066
Equity offering, earlier 2026$18bn
ATM share programme$40bn

Debt to 2066 against depreciating compute is a genuine duration mismatch, and the equity programmes dilute. This is the single biggest change to the investment case in years and it is why the multiple has compressed.

The question the capex raisesRising capital intensity is only a problem if returns lag. Cloud accelerating to 82% with acquisition velocity doubling is evidence they are not lagging — that is demand arriving ahead of capacity, which is the good version of this problem. The bear version is that depreciation and interest compound into the P&L from 2027 while revenue growth normalises. Both readings fit the current data, which is exactly why the stock is 15% off its high rather than at it.
Regulatory — live and accumulatingThe EU Court of Justice made the €4.1bn (~$4.67bn) Android fine final in July, ending an eight-year case; against $119.8bn of quarterly revenue the sum is absorbable, but it strengthens the precedent for cases behind it. The Digital Markets Act has ordered Android data-sharing changes. Nearly 300 French publishers have filed over AI Overviews diverting traffic. US youth-addiction suits against Google, Meta, TikTok and Snap survived motions to dismiss. No single item is material; the direction of travel is.

Why this section exists. Implied volatility is a forecast; realised volatility is a measurement. When they diverge, the options market and the tape disagree about how much room a position needs — and a stop placed on the wrong one is either harvested by noise or wider than the account can carry. Across the eleven names reviewed this weekend, nine had implied below realised, several by more than fifteen points. Default to the measured range unless the two agree.

Reading the table: Stop dist is how far the stop sits from entry as a percentage of the share price — it is not the percentage of the account at risk. Account risk is 1.00% in every row, fixed at $20 on a $2,000 book. Share count is derived from it: shares = $20 ÷ (entry − stop). A wider stop buys fewer shares, not more risk. The only figure that varies with stop distance is position value, which is why the concentration cap and the 1R budget are separate constraints on separate axes.
Two constraints bind before the ratio does. First, $350 open-interest resistance sits 2.4% overhead, so the first target is close and the run to $372 goes straight through it. Second, at this share price a 1R risk of $20 buys a position worth 25–28% of a $2,000 account — inside the 35% cap but concentrated, and any tighter stop pushes it through. This is a name your account can hold only in small size.
If $350 clearsResistance is finite and clearing it on volume inverts the hedging flow. Entry 348.50 with a stop at 336.00 pays 1.88:1 at $372, 3.32:1 at $390, 4.44:1 at $404 — the old high. A trailing exit is what keeps the third of those live.
